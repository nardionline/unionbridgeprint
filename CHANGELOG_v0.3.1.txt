Unionline Bridge v0.3.1 - Bluetooth connection reliability

- Added up to three connection attempts for transient Bluetooth SPP connection failures.
- Added short delays after cancelling discovery and between connection attempts.
- Payload is sent only once after a successful connection. The app deliberately does not
  resend automatically after a write-stage failure because the printer may already have
  received the label, and resending could create duplicate labels.
- Improved error text for connection failures and uncertain write failures.
- TSPL/TSPL2 and ESC/POS payload generation is unchanged.

Physical printer testing is required. If a job fails after connection, check the printer
before manually retrying to avoid a duplicate label.
